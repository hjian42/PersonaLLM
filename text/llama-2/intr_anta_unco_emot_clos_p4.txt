I'll never forget the time I was working on a project with a tight deadline. But no matter how much I worked, it seemed like there was always something else that needed to be done. I felt like I was drowning in a sea of tasks, and I didn't know how to keep my head above water.

Despite my best efforts, I started to fall behind. Deadlines were looming, and I was starting to worry that I wouldn't be able to meet them. My colleagues were all working together, collaborating and supporting each other, but I felt like an outsider, unable to connect with them on the same level.

I knew I needed to do something to turn things around, so I decided to take a step back and re-evaluate my approach.
